      FAQ

      Reasonable questions.

      Is ToolMatch free?

      Yes. It's a personal project rather than a business — no account, no paywall, nothing to cancel.

      How does a tool get listed?

      By hand, one at a time. The test is whether someone with no budget and no prior setup can get something useful out of it, which is why 94 of the 107 have a real free tier.

      Do the listed tools pay you?

      No. There are no affiliate links, no sponsored slots, and no way to buy a better position. Ordering is based on fit alone.

      How current is the pricing?

      Every listing was re-checked on or after 3 September 2026, and each card carries its own date. Treat anything much older than that as a starting point and confirm on the tool's own site before you commit.

      Why only 107 tools?

      Because a list of five hundred is a search problem, not an answer. A short list you can act on is the whole point.

      Can I suggest something?

      Please do. Issues and pull requests on the ToolMatch repository are the fastest route, especially if you've spotted a price that's gone stale.
